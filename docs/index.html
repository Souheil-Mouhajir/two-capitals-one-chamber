Position on the main dimension

The first component of a singular value decomposition of the yearly vote matrix reproduces the single dimension Voeten (2000) found in post Cold War voting, running from a Western group at one end to a counterhegemonic group at the other. Positions carry unit standard deviation and are signed so that the United States is positive. Morocco has sat above Algeria in most years since independence, and both sit far below every Western reference state.

The two positions are easier to read against the whole membership. Each mark below is one state in the year shown, placed on the same dimension. Morocco and Algeria sit inside the large group that clusters away from the Western states, close enough that in most years the eye cannot separate them.

The Sahara on the floor

The file did not leave the Assembly, it changed its name. The annual resolution on the implementation of the Declaration on the Granting of Independence to Colonial Countries and Peoples carries Western Sahara among the non self governing territories. Morocco voted yes on those texts until 2014, abstained twice in the 1990s, and has voted no every year since 2015. Algeria voted yes on every one of them.

Explore the series

The panel recomputes the agreement series for any reference capital, any subject coding in the source file, and either similarity measure. The chance corrected measure of Häge (2011) nets out the Assembly's large consensual majorities: it moves both scores toward zero and leaves their order intact.

Data and methods

The agreement index scores a shared roll call 1 for the same ballot, 0.5 when one abstains and the other votes yes or no, and 0 for opposition, following Bailey et al. (2017, pp. 434 to 436). The chance corrected measure applies those weights to Cohen's (1960) kappa, taking each state's own yes, abstain and no shares on the same roll calls as the baseline, which answers the objection of Häge (2011) that raw similarity rewards states for voting the way almost everyone votes. Positions in Figures 1 to 3 come from the first left singular vector of the column centred vote matrix, with yes coded 1, abstain 0.5 and no 0, missing entries replaced by the roll call mean. Bootstrap intervals resample roll calls with replacement and realign each replicate to the point estimate by sign. Kim and Russett (1996) and Voeten (2000) established the dimensional reading these estimates rely on.

The speech layer uses the General Debate corpus, which holds one prepared address per state per year. It is not the verbatim record of the Assembly's meetings, the A/PV series in which each delegate's floor intervention is transcribed, and none of the exchanges recorded there enter this build.

Three limits are worth stating plainly. The scaling is estimated separately in each year and period, so levels compare within a panel and not across them, unlike the dynamic model of Bailey et al. (2017). Bloc membership uses current rosters, so the caucus measure applies today's borders to earlier decades. The Sahara table is built by string matching on the resolution record, which will miss votes filed under other titles.
